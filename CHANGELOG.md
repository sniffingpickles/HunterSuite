# HunterSuite 4.5.0

Ammo restock: top up at any vendor that sells your ammo, and find the nearest one when you run low.

### New
- **Ammo restock:** at a vendor that sells ammo for your weapon, a panel beside the vendor window buys the best you can use, up to the amount you keep (1200 by default) or a full quiver. It buys whole stacks, stays within your bag space and money, and puts the new ammo on when it beats what you wear. Settings are under Ammo restock, including topping up without the click.
- **Nearest ammo vendor:** `/hs ammo`, or a click on the ammo counter, pins the nearest vendor of your faction that sells the best ammo you can use, with the arrow to follow. It knows 125 vendors, including the new ones on Zephras Isle, in Riverglades and on Mount Hyjal, and remembers any ammo vendor you open.
